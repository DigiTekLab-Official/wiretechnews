## Enterprise Definition

Under the GDPR (Article 6), no enterprise may collect, store, or process personal data without first establishing a valid **Lawful Basis**. This is the fundamental legal gateway for data operations. The chosen basis must be determined before processing begins, explicitly documented in the organization's Record of Processing Activities (RoPA), and communicated to the data subject via privacy notices.

There is no hierarchy among the bases; the appropriate choice depends entirely on the specific operational context and the relationship with the data subject.

## The Six Lawful Bases

Enterprise data operations must strictly align with one of the following foundations:

1.  **Consent:** The data subject has given clear, unambiguous, and easily withdrawn affirmative agreement for a specific processing purpose (e.g., tracking cookies, marketing newsletters).
2.  **Contract:** Processing is necessary to fulfill a contract with the data subject, or because they have asked for specific steps to be taken before entering a contract (e.g., processing payment details to ship a product).
3.  **Legal Obligation:** Processing is necessary to comply with a common law or statutory obligation (e.g., sharing salary data with national tax authorities).
4.  **Vital Interests:** Processing is critical to protecting someone's life (typically utilized in emergency medical contexts).
5.  **Public Task:** Processing is necessary to perform a task in the public interest or for official functions (largely restricted to public authorities).
6.  **Legitimate Interests:** The most flexible, yet highly scrutinized basis. Processing is necessary for the enterprise's legitimate interests (or those of a third party), provided those interests are not overridden by the data subject's fundamental rights and freedoms. 

> **Operational Note:** Relying on Legitimate Interests requires conducting and documenting a formal Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) to prove that the enterprise's operational need balances lawfully against user privacy.
